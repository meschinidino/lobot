agreement (0.85) and reaches knob; rhyming fails agreement

## Known gaps

- Model-written rubrics are unreliable (1/2 pass). The
  meta-prompt needs rubric-shape guidance ("write a yes/no
  question the judge answers about the reply"); that is new
  work, done after this report, not during the gate.
- Model-written pos/neg drift toward topics ("describe a crime
  scene") instead of voice; contrast still works, but the knob
  may steer topic, not style. The noir knob steers crime-scene
  noir, which matches its description.
- Labels by the backend agent, not Dino. A labeling UI + Dino
  labels would strengthen the claim (UI lane).
- The 20-label gate is coarse (0.85 = 17/20; one label flips
  the verdict). Same coarseness as O1 (12 labels); reported,
  not hidden.
