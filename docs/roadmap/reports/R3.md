## Method notes (deviations from the spec, with reasons)

- Word floor on steered_p (>= 1e-4), not base_p. Content
  words score base_p 1e-7..1e-9 on neutral prompts, so the
  base_p floor admitted zero of 378 candidates. The steered_p
  floor keeps the anti-noise intent: the word must become
  genuinely likely.
- Agreement does not gate `passes`. Exact-stem matching
  undercounts real agreement (fires "rained"/"drizzly" vs up
  "rain"). It is reported and breaks ties; the winner's
  contexts got a human read.
- The lift screen covered the whole pool (spec-literal),
  about 6,000 prefills in 46 minutes.
